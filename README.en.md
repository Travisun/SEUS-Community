## What is Nssnap

Nssnap Community (nssnap.com) is a place to talk about hosting, domain names, servers, software and super intelligence (SI/AI), plus study, work and life beyond tech. Whether you are registering your first domain or running hundreds of servers, every exchange you start gets a serious answer.

The house rule is simple: beginners can ask anything, and someone will answer. Good questions don't wait long. Good answers stay, like snapshots, ready for the next person who runs into the same problem. The community favors altruistic content, because we believe what people write is worth reading more than AI-generated pages.

Posts are auto-translated, so friends from different countries can talk in the same thread in their own language. Sign in with GitHub, Google, Discord, Linux.do or Cloudflare, or register with an email address.

## The name

Nssnap comes from NS and Snap.

| Part | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| N | New, and the N of Domain Name. Everything starts with a good domain |
| S | Server, Super Intelligence, Software. Today's hottest tech topics, in one letter |
| Snap | A snapshot, and "in a snap". Ask, talk, find your people, all in one place |

S also stands for every S friend around you: Student, Scientist, Sysadmin, Software Engineer, Singer, Screenwriter and more. Tech is the main line here. People are what make it.

## What we talk about

Main line, tech: domain names, hosting, servers, software, super intelligence (SI/AI). Newcomer questions get taken seriously, and deep-dive discussions find their readers.

Second line, life: study, work, relationships, travel, personal opinions. Life deserves a serious conversation too.

## What the community offers

**Talking and keeping**

- Long posts and short updates share one timeline. Markdown rendering done right: KaTeX math, syntax highlighting and Mermaid diagrams are part of the text, not screenshots
- Discussions stay searchable and subscribable, with RSS across the whole site
- Follow, like, comment, mention and direct message. Sign-ups through invite links automatically follow the inviter, so you have followers on day one
- Export everything with one click. Every word you write belongs to you

**Member perks**

- Free second-level domains through the PublicFreeSuffix project, with NS delegation so your DNS stays under your control
- Free global DNS resolution powered by Bunny.net, managed from the community panel
- Nssnap Coin (TGC): earn points by showing up, contributing and helping others, then tip friends, send red envelopes or trade services in the community store
- Member name colors and effects, storage space and community merchandise
- Installable as a PWA, native apps on the way

**Shared governance**

- Official members hold proposal and voting rights. The community decides where it goes
- Moderators are always wanted, with weekly points as compensation
- Everyone can report content that breaks the rules, and handled reports earn points too

## Who should join

- Beginners registering a first domain, renting a first VPS or self-hosting for the first time. No question is too small here, and your first post will get a reply
- Engineers who live with servers, domains and deployment pipelines. Your experience stays searchable and citable instead of evaporating in a group chat
- Students, researchers and anyone chasing the AI frontier. Math and diagrams are first-class citizens, and deep discussions have readers
- Designers, writers and indie makers. Post long or short, keep your formatting and your voice

And if you just want a quiet place to watch people tinker, come on in. Someone will look up and say, hey, take a seat.

1. Answers. Questions get answered, answers have quality, and it is written into how the community works, not just said

## Why join

There are plenty of places to talk tech. Each one asks you to trade something away.

| Where people go today | What usually goes wrong |
| --- | --- |
| Big content platforms | Beginner questions sink, and answer quality follows the traffic |
| Niche hosting forums | Topics stay narrow, so software, AI and life have nowhere to sit |
| Group chats | Fast replies, but messages vanish, and that answer from three years ago is gone |
| AI assistants | Instant answers, no peers, and nobody to double-check them |

Nssnap tries to give you both, the speed of a reply and the depth of an archive. We don't run on traffic, we run on responses. Good questions shouldn't wait.
